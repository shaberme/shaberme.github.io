About Me

Welcome! My name is Scott Habermehl. I am a researcher at Caltech in the group of Prof. Michael Roukes. My current work is principally aimed at utilizing nanoelectromechanical systems (NEMS) to build networks of coupled oscillators to study their fundamental physics and to leverage their exotic dynamics for analog computation. I work on all areas of the project including device simulation, design, fabrication, and characterization; electronics design and integration; programming and control of experimental systems; numerical and theoretical analysis of coupled oscillator dynamics; and simulation of analog computing methods. My broader research interests include nanomechanics, RF electronic systems, nonlinear dynamics, network science, analog/neuromorphic/oscillatory computing, and novel device physics.
